# Iordis

A student note-taking and study product in development.

**[Website](https://iordis.com)** · **[Daniel Iravani](https://danieliravani.com)**

## The project

Iordis is being developed around a student's semester: course material, notes, study sessions, flashcards, and planning. The product direction connects mobile capture with the places students organize and study their notes.

My current work includes the public product site, account flows, the sample study workspace, and the development of the student application.

## Current public surfaces

- A responsive product site and consistent visual identity.
- Account registration, sign-in, account settings, and sign-out.
- A sample workspace showing course notes, flashcards, and a study plan.
- Public brand assets and product information.

## Product direction

The broader product is being developed to organize courses and study material across web, desktop, and mobile surfaces. School-platform imports, transcription, and approved lecture-capture workflows are development goals; the public sample workspace demonstrates the intended experience.

## Technical work

- React, TypeScript, and Vite for the public web experience.
- Clerk for the account system.
- Swift and SwiftUI work in the Apple application checkout.
- Responsive layouts, keyboard interaction, and verification of saved account state.
